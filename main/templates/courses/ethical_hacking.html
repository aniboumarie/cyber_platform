Ethical Hacking & Penetration Testing

Master the art of offensive security to defend like a pro.

Course Overview

This course immerses you in the world of ethical hacking and penetration testing, teaching you to think like an attacker to identify and exploit vulnerabilities. You'll gain hands-on experience with the tools and methodologies used by security professionals to assess and secure information systems. Our approach is practical, focusing on real-world techniques and ethical considerations.

From reconnaissance and scanning to exploitation and post-exploitation, you will learn the complete lifecycle of a penetration test. This course is your gateway to a career in offensive security.

Learning Objectives

  • Understand the legal and ethical frameworks governing penetration testing.
  • Master various reconnaissance techniques to gather information about target systems.
  • Proficiently use scanning tools (e.g., Nmap, Nessus) to identify open ports, services, and vulnerabilities.
  • Learn common web application, network, and system exploitation techniques.
  • Develop skills in post-exploitation, including privilege escalation and maintaining access.
  • Effectively document and report findings from a penetration test.

Who Is This Course For?

Aspiring penetration testers, security analysts, network engineers, system administrators, and IT professionals looking to specialize in offensive security and vulnerability assessment.

Prerequisites

Solid understanding of networking (TCP/IP), operating systems (Windows and Linux), and familiarity with command-line interfaces. Basic scripting knowledge (e.g., Python, Bash) is advantageous but not strictly required.

Estimated Duration

Approximately 45-50 hours of study, including intensive hands-on labs and practical exercises designed to simulate real-world scenarios.

Key Topics Covered

  • Introduction to Ethical Hacking and Legal Frameworks
  • Footprinting, Reconnaissance, and Information Gathering
  • Scanning Networks and Enumeration Techniques
  • Vulnerability Analysis and Assessment
  • System Hacking (Malware, Password Attacks, Privilege Escalation)
  • Web Application Hacking (SQL Injection, XSS, CSRF)
  • Wireless Network Penetration Testing
  • Social Engineering Tactics and Countermeasures
  • Evading IDS, Firewalls, and Honeypots
  • Reporting and Post-Engagement Activities
