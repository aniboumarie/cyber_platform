Incident Response & Digital Forensics

Effectively manage security incidents and uncover digital evidence like an expert.

Course Overview

This course equips you with the critical skills needed to prepare for, respond to, and recover from cybersecurity incidents. You will also delve into the fundamentals of digital forensics, learning how to collect, preserve, analyze, and present digital evidence in a legally sound manner. We cover the entire incident response lifecycle and forensic investigation process, from initial detection to post-incident analysis and reporting.

Through practical scenarios and hands-on exercises, you'll gain experience with industry-standard tools and techniques used by incident response teams and forensic investigators.

Learning Objectives

  • Understand the phases of the incident response lifecycle (Preparation, Identification, Containment, Eradication, Recovery, Lessons Learned).
  • Develop and implement effective incident response plans and playbooks.
  • Master techniques for identifying, containing, and eradicating security threats.
  • Learn the principles of digital forensics, including evidence handling and chain of custody.
  • Perform forensic analysis on operating systems (Windows, Linux), networks, and mobile devices.
  • Utilize common forensic tools for data acquisition, analysis, and reporting.

Who Is This Course For?

Aspiring incident responders, digital forensic analysts, cybersecurity managers, IT professionals, law enforcement personnel, and legal professionals seeking to understand incident handling and digital evidence.

Prerequisites

A good understanding of operating systems, networking concepts, and general cybersecurity principles is required. Familiarity with command-line tools and basic IT troubleshooting will be beneficial.

Estimated Duration

Approximately 40-45 hours of study, combining theoretical knowledge with practical lab work focused on incident scenarios and forensic investigations.

Key Topics Covered

Introduction to Incident Response and Handling +

Define what constitutes a security incident and understand the importance of a structured incident response process. This module introduces the goals of incident handling and the typical challenges faced by organizations.

Developing an Incident Response Plan and Team +

Learn the critical components of an effective incident response plan (IRP) and how to establish and train an incident response team (IRT). This includes defining roles, responsibilities, communication protocols, and escalation procedures.

Incident Detection, Triage, and Prioritization +

Explore various methods for detecting security incidents, from automated alerts via SIEMs and IDS/IPS to manual reporting. Learn how to triage incidents effectively, assess their impact, and prioritize them for response.

Containment, Eradication, and Recovery Strategies +

Master the core phases of incident handling: strategies for containing the incident to prevent further damage, methods for eradicating the threat from affected systems, and processes for securely recovering systems and data to normal operations.

Post-Incident Activities and Lessons Learned +

Understand the importance of post-incident analysis, including conducting lessons learned reviews, updating the IRP, and implementing improvements to prevent future occurrences. This module also covers reporting requirements.

Fundamentals of Digital Forensics and Evidence Handling +

Introduce the principles of digital forensics, focusing on the proper collection, preservation, and handling of digital evidence to maintain its integrity and admissibility. Learn about the chain of custody and legal considerations.

Operating System Forensics (Windows, Linux) +

Delve into forensic analysis techniques specific to Windows and Linux operating systems. This includes examining file systems, memory dumps, registry hives (Windows), system logs, and user activity to uncover evidence of compromise.

Network Forensics and Traffic Analysis +

Learn how to capture and analyze network traffic (e.g., using Wireshark) to identify malicious communications, reconstruct events, and trace attacker activity across the network. Understand logs from firewalls, proxies, and other network devices.

Malware Analysis in Incident Response +

Gain an introduction to malware analysis techniques used during incident response, including static and dynamic analysis, to understand malware behavior, capabilities, and indicators of compromise (IOCs).

Forensic Reporting and Expert Witness Testimony Basics +

Learn how to document forensic findings clearly and comprehensively in a forensic report. This module also touches upon the basics of presenting technical evidence and potentially serving as an expert witness.
